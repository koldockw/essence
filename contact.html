GOOD TO KNOW

A few quick
answers.

How do I choose a fragrance?

Start with a note you enjoy or try our three-step scent finder. Your suggestions are saved for your next visit.

Which bottle size should I pick?

A 30 ml bottle is a nice first discovery. Larger sizes suit a fragrance you wear often. Each product page lists the available volumes.

How do promo codes work?

Copy a code from Offers, open your bag and paste it into the promo field. You’ll see your saving immediately. Codes cannot be combined.

Is delivery included?

For the demo, delivery is 2,000 ₸. It becomes free at a subtotal of 80,000 ₸, or with FREESHIP. No real order is dispatched.

Where is my collection saved?

Accounts, favourites and demo orders are saved on the browser and device you use. They do not synchronise between devices.

Can I place a real order?

ESSENCE is a university concept store. Checkout creates a demo order in your browser. It never charges you or ships a product.
